TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It estimates the total calories you burn in a day, including resting needs, daily movement and exercise. Enter your BMR and activity level below. Don't know your BMR? Use the BMR calculator first.

How TDEE is calculated

TDEE is estimated by multiplying your BMR by an activity factor: 1.2 (sedentary), 1.375 (lightly active), 1.55 (moderately active), 1.725 (very active) or 1.9 (extra active). For a full walkthrough, read how to calculate your TDEE step by step.

What to do with your TDEE

Eating around your TDEE helps maintain your weight. Many people adjust it slightly for their goal using the calorie goal planner, or get a one-step estimate with the daily calorie calculator. Learn the difference in BMR vs TDEE explained.
