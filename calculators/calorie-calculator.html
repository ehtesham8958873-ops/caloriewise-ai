How your daily calories are estimated

First, your BMR is estimated with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation. It is then multiplied by an activity factor to get your maintenance calories (your TDEE). For weight loss or gain, a gentle adjustment of about 500 kcal per day is applied. Read the full explanation in our daily calorie needs guide.

Tips for using your result

Treat the number as a starting point, not a strict rule. Real needs vary with metabolism, health and lifestyle. Compare how you feel and how your weight changes over a few weeks, then adjust. To understand the difference between resting and total needs, see BMR vs TDEE explained.

This tool gives general educational estimates, not medical advice. See our disclaimer. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional if you are under 18, pregnant, breastfeeding, have a medical condition, or have a history of disordered eating.
