How the plan is calculated

About 7,700 calories is commonly used as the energy equivalent of 1 kg of body weight. Your daily difference from maintenance is the weekly pace multiplied by 7,700 and divided by 7. The timeline is your weight difference divided by the weekly pace. These are rough estimates, because real progress varies. See the daily calorie needs guide for more.

This tool gives general educational estimates, not medical advice. See our disclaimer. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional if you are under 18, pregnant, breastfeeding, have a medical condition, or have a history of disordered eating.
