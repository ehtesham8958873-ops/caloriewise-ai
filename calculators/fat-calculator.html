How fat intake is calculated

Fat provides about 9 calories per gram. Your calories are multiplied by the chosen percentage range, then divided by 9 to give grams. Learn more in our guide to protein, carbs and fat.

This tool gives general educational estimates, not medical advice. People with medical conditions should plan their fat intake with a healthcare professional. See our disclaimer.
