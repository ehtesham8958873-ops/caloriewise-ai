How protein needs are estimated

Protein targets are usually expressed in grams per kilogram of body weight. Typical ranges are about 0.8–1.0 g/kg for general health, 1.2–1.6 g/kg for active people, and up to 1.6–2.2 g/kg for strength training. Your result is your weight multiplied by these values. Learn more in our guide to protein, carbs and fat.

This tool gives general educational estimates, not medical advice. People with kidney disease or other medical conditions should check their protein intake with a healthcare professional. See our disclaimer.
