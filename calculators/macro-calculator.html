How macros are calculated

Protein and carbohydrates provide about 4 calories per gram, and fat provides about 9 calories per gram. Your calories are multiplied by each percentage, then divided by those values to give grams. Learn more in our guide to protein, carbs and fat.

Choosing your percentages

There is no single correct split. The default of 30% protein, 40% carbs and 30% fat is just a balanced starting point. Your needs depend on your activity, preferences and health. If you only want a protein target based on body weight, use the protein calculator.
