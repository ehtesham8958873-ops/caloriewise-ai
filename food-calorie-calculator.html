Free Food Calorie Calculator

Estimate calories in common foods and serving sizes with this free online food calorie calculator. Choose an item, enter the number of servings and calculate an approximate calorie total. Your entries are processed in your browser.

Choose a food and serving amount to see your estimate.

The calorie value shown for each option is an approximate reference value for the stated serving size.

How to use the Food Calorie Calculator

  1. Select a food from the list.
  2. Enter how many servings you ate. You can use fractions such as 0.5 or 1.5.
  3. Select “Estimate food calories” to calculate the approximate total.

How the estimate is calculated

The calculator multiplies the reference calories for one listed serving by the number of servings. For example, two 100 g servings of cooked rice at approximately 130 kcal each provide an estimated 260 kcal.
