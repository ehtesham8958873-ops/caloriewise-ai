Free Meal Calorie Calculator

Add the calorie values for foods in your meal to estimate the total. This free meal calorie calculator lets you enter up to four items, with optional food names, and adds the calories in your browser.

Enter the calories for each food item in your meal. You can leave unused rows at zero.

Enter the calorie values for your meal.

How to use the Meal Calorie Calculator

  1. Enter the name of each food item if you want it shown in the result.
  2. Enter the calorie value for each item. Use a food label, trusted nutrition database or your own food calorie estimate.
  3. Leave unused calorie fields at zero, then select “Calculate meal total”.

How the meal total is calculated

The calculator adds the calorie values entered for up to four food items. It does not identify foods or measure portions automatically, so the accuracy depends on the calorie values you provide.
